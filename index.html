Search and AI that are fast, practical, and built to scale.

I am a Research Director at the Institute of Information Science and Technologies (ISTI) of the Italian National Research Council in Pisa, where I lead the High Performance Computing Laboratory. My research is about making information retrieval and AI systems efficient: faster ranking, leaner representations, lower energy, without giving up quality.

Research

Three areas of work

01

Efficient search and ranking

How fast can a search engine rank, and at what cost? Learning to rank with tree ensembles (QuickScorer), early-exit strategies, caching and compressed indexes, hardware acceleration on SIMD and FPGAs, and efficient dense retrieval.

    All papers in this area →
    02

    Neural and conversational search

    What makes neural representations work, and how do we know a system is doing well? Dimension importance in dense retrieval, conversational search, query performance prediction, evaluation, and LLMs for ranking and judging relevance.

      All papers in this area →
      03

      Queries, users and recommendation

      What do people's searches tell us about what they need? Query log mining, query suggestion and search tasks, recommendation and personalization, from tourist itineraries to expert finding in community question answering.

        All papers in this area →

        The lab

        High Performance Computing Laboratory

        The HPC Lab at ISTI-CNR develops scalable data management and information retrieval methods for computationally and data-intensive applications, from indexing, retrieval and semantic enrichment to large-scale analytics, distributed processing and AI-based decision support. We build systems that work under real-world constraints: large data volumes, low latency, limited resources, distributed infrastructures and privacy-aware processing.

        • Efficient, distributed and resource-aware AI. Compression, quantization, distillation, caching and adaptive inference for LLMs and agentic applications across cloud, edge and device.
        • Efficient information retrieval and search. Indexing and querying of dense and sparse embeddings, vector search, ranking, re-ranking and RAG pipelines.
        • Cloud-edge continuum. Decentralized, AI-enhanced orchestration, service placement and scheduling across heterogeneous infrastructures.
        • Mobility, social data and semantic enrichment. Semantic trajectories, urban dynamics and LLM-based understanding of text and social media.
